Privacy Policy

This page explains, in plain language, how Keedl is intended to handle information from services you choose to connect.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Services you connect

You may choose to connect external services to Keedl. A connection requires your authorization. You can decide whether to connect a service and can remove a connection or revoke its access through the available Keedl controls and, where applicable, through the service itself.

How information is used

Information accessed through a connection is used to provide the features of Keedl, such as organizing and helping you find information. The specific information and features depend on the service connected and the features available in Keedl.

Your information and privacy

Your information belongs to you. Keedl is designed with privacy and user control in mind. Where applicable, processing may take place locally. The actual handling of information depends on the features you use and the services you connect. Keedl does not sell personal data.

Third-party services

External services, including Google, operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Those terms continue to apply when you use those services or authorize a connection to them through Keedl.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact the Keedl team. An official contact address has not yet been configured for this site.
